01 / REVISION LOG

From basemap to spot on.

A condensed record of what changed, what the datalogs revealed, and how the car evolved through each ETunez revision.

R1
JUNE 30, 2020

Foundation & Mechanical Discovery

The custom basemap introduced major changes across fueling, ignition, cam angle strategy, WOT transitions, IAT compensation, VTC engagement, throttle mapping and rev-hang behavior. The Hybrid CAI setup started in Speed Density at WOT, with VTC engagement smoothed at 3,850 RPM, launch control set to 5,000 RPM and redline set to 8,400 RPM.

HYBRID SD/MAPVTC 3850 RPMLC 5000 RPMREDLINE 8400 RPM
R1 Discovery — O2 / Defouler

Datalogging exposed a 2–4 second AFR delay and fuel-trim swings reported at +46 / -20%. ETunez suspected sensor placement. The shop later found the defouler had been installed incorrectly, contributing to the rough idle and slow sensor response. The installation was corrected and a fresh datalog was submitted.

R2
JULY 23, 2020

Speed Density & Response

With the mechanical issue corrected, the logs finally contained the information ETunez needed. Speed Density tables were calibrated and centered against the ECU reference tables, while the process moved into dedicated WOT logging from roughly 2,000 RPM to redline.

SD TABLESWOT LOGGINGFUELINGRESPONSE

Driver impression: A major jump in responsiveness and drivability — the car had become a joy to drive.

R3
JULY 24, 2020

PT/WOT Refinement & Power

Further trim adjustments targeted the remaining on/off-throttle hesitation while both part-throttle and WOT calibration started dialing in. Response sharpened again and ETunez noted additional power gains.

PTWOTFUEL TRIMSTRANSITIONS

Driver impression: Most or all hesitation was gone. The car felt substantially more refined, with stronger low- and high-end power.

R4
JULY 27, 2020

Fine-Tuning & Stability

The calibration was becoming stable and increasingly ideal. Adjustments had narrowed to roughly 2–4%, aimed at improving STFT and throttle response. ETunez described the knock counts as “not too bad” and the tune as making good progress.

2–4% CORRECTIONSSTFTTHROTTLE RESPONSESTABILITY

Driver impression: Idle had reached the sweet spot, cruising improved, and the upper-RPM pull became increasingly noticeable.

R5
JULY 28, 2020

Near-Perfect Calibration

ETunez reported that the previous changes had taken near perfectly. Overall fueling and AFRs were near spot on and very stable, with excellent fueling feedback. The knock/ignition threshold was also dialing in near perfectly; only small PT/WOT corrections remained to reduce variation.

AFR NEAR TARGETSTABLE FUELINGPT/WOTFINAL CORRECTIONS

Driver impression: Excellent idle and drivability remained intact, with especially strong upper-RPM performance.

R6
JULY 29, 2020

Final Calibration — Spot On

After reviewing the fuel trims, knock counts, AFRs, ignition behavior and averages, ETunez called the results spot on. R6 showed excellent stability, consistency and ideal AFRs. The final work focused on the last 1% of WOT/PT fueling and environmental transitions, with all remaining adjustments in the 1–2% range.

IDEAL AFRs1–2% FINAL ADJUSTMENTSSTABLECOMPLETE
Calibration Complete

ETunez reported no more changes were needed at that time unless the vehicle's modifications changed.
