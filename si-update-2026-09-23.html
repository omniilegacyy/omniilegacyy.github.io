SI UPDATE / SEPTEMBER 23, 2026

Back on the Road.

Two days after isolating the no-start problem to the grounding system, the Si got its new battery and chassis ground cables. The result: it started right up and the K20 was back to sounding and responding exactly the way it should.

STATUS • RUNNING LIKE A CHAMP

The Ground Fix

On September 21, the forced jump-start helped point the diagnosis toward the ground cables. On the 23rd, I replaced both the battery ground cable and the chassis ground cable. The car started immediately. The radio was back, the engine sounded great, RPM response and power felt spot on, and the power-steering issue I experienced during the earlier forced jump-start was gone.

REPLACEDBattery Ground Cable

New cable installed as part of the grounding-system repair.

REPLACEDChassis Ground Cable

Ground path refreshed and the no-start condition resolved.

One More Mystery

The first test drive revealed a completely different problem: 4th, 5th, 6th and reverse were not engaging even though the engine and electrical systems were behaving normally. With help from my wife, she rowed through the gears from inside the car while I watched the linkage in the engine bay.

That made the problem obvious. The replacement battery ground cable was much larger than the original routing and was physically interfering with the shifter mechanism, preventing it from reaching part of its travel. I repositioned the cable to clear the linkage and the gears — including reverse — came back.

FIX CONFIRMED

Ground cables replaced. Strong clean start. Electrical and steering behavior normal. Shifter interference identified and corrected. And yes — I could finally put the car in reverse and park it. 😎
